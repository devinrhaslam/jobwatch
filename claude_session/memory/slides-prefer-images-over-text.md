On 2026-10-13, while adapting the email-service deck for a mixed technical and non-technical panel, Devin said: "I prefer images to text on these slides", gave the example "little icons for the return lifecycle would do", and "Less detail on these slides is better than more detail, I can talk through everything and answer questions". He also pushed back on a proposed slide listing which app sends which email as "pretty irrelevant".

**Why:** he presents live and carries the detail verbally; dense slides compete with him.
The same day he asked for three more cuts: remove the stat tiles from the About me slide ("I don't want to talk about all my projects"), remove all speaker notes because he would be sending the deck to someone for advice, and take "the architect" off the delivery slide because "it makes it seem like someone else lead the project not me".

**How to apply:** for his decks, default to icon-led layouts with a short label and at most one line per item. Don't add speaker notes unless he asks; give the detail in chat instead. Don't add a slide just to document a fact he can say aloud. Frame delivery slides around what he drove, and don't give other people's roles a card of their own. My interpretation: this applies to slides he presents, not to study sheets or written prep. Related: [[deep-dive-deck-story-order]], [[email-service-deep-dive-project]].
