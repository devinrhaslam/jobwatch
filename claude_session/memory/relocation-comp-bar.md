Modeled 2026-09-20 from Devin's real monthly budget ($10,174/mo, 31 lines) against
2026 tax law. To hold his **current savings rate constant** he needs roughly:

| Where | Target total comp |
|---|---|
| Manhattan, 1BR, no car | **$364k** |
| Brooklyn, 2BR, no car | **$367k** |
| San Francisco, 1BR, no car | **$331k** |
| San Francisco, 2BR + one car | **$387k** |

Break-even floors (savings drop to zero) are ~$270k NYC / ~$239k SF. Same-%-of-gross
targets run $354k–$440k.

**Key structural facts that drive this:**
- Comp is **$220k base + 30% bonus, all cash, no equity** — so a headline-equal RSU
  offer is not equal. Always get the base/bonus/equity split.
- **$1,151 mortgage** (PITI) on the Richmond house; plan is to **keep it and rent it
  out** (~$2,100/mo gross → ~$592/mo net after mgmt + vacancy).
- Partner does **not work** and Devin explicitly asked to model on his income alone.
- ~$2,600/mo of the budget is house-and-car overhead a city deletes (home maint,
  lawn, water, vehicle tax, gas, auto insurance, future-vehicle sinking fund).
- Pets — 2 dogs, cat, fish tank ($750/mo) — are the real constraint on NYC housing,
  more than the money.
- **Filing status barely matters** (<$13k swing): joint filing cuts tax but raises
  the savings bar it's measured against, and the two mostly cancel.

**Why:** Devin was getting wild numbers from Google because COL indexes compare
averages, and his budget is far from average (tiny mortgage, near-zero restaurant
spend, heavy pets/fitness).

**How to apply:** when screening roles in NYC/SF, treat **$365k** as the match-my-life
number and anything under ~$270k as a pay cut in real terms. This supersedes the flat
~$330k figure in [[capital-one-offer-2026-08]] for out-of-market roles — that number
was Richmond-based. Feeds [[job-search-targeting-criteria]]. Interactive model:
