Chime Senior SWE, Growth (final loop 2026-09-23/24) — rejected 2026-09-25 by recruiter Alex Cheney. Technicals "really solid"; reason given was that hiring manager Brian saw a backend/platform fit, not what his Growth team needs. Devin talked mostly about a backend project in the HM round and Brian never asked about full-stack work, though Devin has a lot of it. Devin replied asking to be considered for backend/platform roles (and mentioned full-stack experience); Alex had offered to reach out if a better role opens.

**Why:** "Fit" rejections can be partly a soft excuse, but the HM round is where the manager decides whether your background matches the team's work, and they judge from the stories you pick. Interviewers don't reliably ask about the rest.

**How to apply:** In future HM rounds, choose the lead story to match the team (product/growth → user-facing, experiment-driven, full-stack work) and proactively bridge to it. Related: [[email-service-deep-dive-project]], [[system-design-interview-weak-spot]].
