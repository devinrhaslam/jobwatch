Devin is interviewing at **Audible** (confirmed 2026-08-24) and prepping the
behavioral rounds. Audible is an Amazon subsidiary and runs the standard Amazon
loop for tech roles — LP-based behavioral questions and a Bar Raiser — even
though audiblecareers.com frames everything around Audible's own People
Principles and never mentions Amazon LPs or the Bar Raiser.

Audible publishes **five** People Principles, not six: Be Customer Obsessed;
Imagine & Invent Before They Ask; Articulate the Possible & Move Fast to Make It
Real; Study & Draw Inspiration from Culture & Technology; Activate Caring.
Devin recalled six — if a recruiter deck lists a sixth, believe the deck.

**Why:** he asked whether prepping only the People Principles was sufficient. It
is not — the five leave **Ownership, Dive Deep, Have Backbone (Disagree and
Commit), Earn Trust, and Frugality** completely unprobed, and the first three
are the most heavily weighted LPs for SDE candidates.

**How to apply:** the strategy is one story bank of ~10–12 stories indexed to
the 16 LPs, relabeled in People Principles language when answering — not two
separate prep tracks. Audible's own careers page mandates STAR and explicitly
says use "I," not "we." Related: [[datadog-code-review-round]],
[[system-design-interview-weak-spot]], [[job-search-targeting-criteria]].
